## Welcome

Welcome to the **CCI Department Guidance System**! This intelligent career assessment platform is designed to help you discover which Computer and Computing Information (CCI) department aligns best with your interests, skills, and career aspirations.

### What This System Does

The CCI Department Guidance System analyzes your responses to a comprehensive questionnaire and provides:

- **Personalized Department Recommendations** - Match percentages for all 6 CCI departments
- **Detailed Persona Profile** - A personality badge that describes your computing style
- **Career Insights** - Explanations of why specific departments fit your profile
- **Comparative Analysis** - Side-by-side comparison of multiple departments

### Available Departments

The system evaluates your fit across these 6 departments:

1. **Software Engineering (SWE)** - Building scalable software systems
2. **Computer Science (CS)** - Theoretical foundations and algorithms
3. **Information Technology (IT)** - Infrastructure and technical support
4. **Information Systems (IS)** - Business technology integration
5. **Statistics (STAT)** - Data analysis and mathematical modeling
6. **Information Science (ISC)** - Information management and human-computer interaction

## Taking the Assessment

### Step 1: Start Your Assessment

1. Click the **"Start Assessment"** button on the homepage
2. You'll be presented with 20 carefully designed questions
3. The assessment takes approximately **8-10 minutes** to complete

### Step 2: Answer Questions Honestly

**Question Categories:**

The 20 questions cover five key areas:

1. **Interests** - What excites you about computing?
2. **Skills** - What are your natural strengths?
3. **Learning Style** - How do you prefer to learn?
4. **Career Goals** - Where do you see yourself working?
5. **Problem-Solving** - How do you approach challenges?

**Tips for Best Results:**

✅ **DO:**
- Answer honestly based on your genuine preferences
- Take your time reading each question
- Think about real situations you've experienced
- Choose the option that resonates most with you

❌ **DON'T:**
- Rush through the questions
- Try to guess "correct" answers
- Answer based on what others expect
- Leave questions unanswered

### Step 3: Navigate Through Questions

**Navigation Controls:**

- **"Previous" Button** - Go back to review or change answers
- **"Next" Button** - Move to the next question (only available after answering)
- **Progress Bar** - Shows how many questions you've completed (e.g., "5/20")

**Example Question:**

```
Question 5 of 20

Which of these activities interests you most?

○ Building web applications and mobile apps
○ Analyzing data to find patterns and insights
○ Designing user-friendly interfaces
○ Setting up and maintaining computer networks
○ Writing algorithms to solve complex problems
○ Managing databases and information systems

                [← Previous]    [Next →]
```

### Step 4: Submit Your Assessment

1. After answering all 20 questions, click **"Submit Assessment"**
2. The system processes your responses (takes 2-3 seconds)
3. You'll be automatically redirected to your personalized results page

**Note:** Once submitted, you cannot change your answers. If you need to retake the assessment, start a new one from the homepage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: How long does the assessment take?**  
A: Most students complete it in 8-10 minutes.

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**  
A: No, the assessment is completely anonymous and requires no registration.

**Q: Can I save my progress and come back later?**  
A: Currently, you must complete the assessment in one session. Plan for 10-15 minutes of uninterrupted time.

**Q: Can I retake the assessment?**  
A: Yes! You can retake it as many times as you like. Results may vary based on your responses.

**Q: Is my data private?**  
A: Yes. We don't collect personal information unless you voluntarily provide your name/email. All responses are stored securely.

### Assessment Questions

**Q: What if I can't decide between two answer options?**  
A: Choose the one that feels most natural or that you've experienced more often.

**Q: Are there "right" or "wrong" answers?**  
A: No! The assessment is designed to find the best fit for YOUR unique profile.

**Q: Can I change my answers after submitting?**  
A: No, but you can retake the entire assessment if you want different results.

**Q: What if I don't like any of the answer options?**  
A: Choose the option that's closest to your preference. The system considers patterns across all 20 questions.

### Results Questions

**Q: What if my top match isn't what I expected?**  
A: Results are based on your responses. Consider exploring the recommended department with an open mind, or retake the assessment.

**Q: What if two departments have similar scores?**  
A: Use the "Compare Departments" feature to see detailed differences, or consult with an academic advisor.

**Q: Can I get a copy of my results?**  
A: You can take screenshots or write down your results. Print functionality coming soon!

**Q: How accurate are the recommendations?**  
A: The system has been validated with student feedback and departmental data. However, it's a guidance tool, not a final decision.

### Technical Questions

**Q: The assessment isn't loading. What should I do?**  
A: 
1. Refresh your browser (Ctrl+F5 or Cmd+Shift+R)
2. Clear your browser cache
3. Try a different browser
4. Check your internet connection

**Q: I'm getting an error message. Help!**  
A: Take a screenshot of the error and contact technical support (see below).

**Q: Does the system work on mobile devices?**  
A: Yes! The interface is fully responsive and works on smartphones and tablets.

**Q: Why is the page loading slowly?**  
A: This may be due to internet connection speed. The assessment requires a stable connection.
