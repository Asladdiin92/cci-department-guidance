## College of Computing and Informatics (CCI) — Overview

**Background:**

The College of Computing and Informatics was established in April 2008 by bringing together
three departments — Computer Science, Management Information System and Information Studies —
that formed the then Faculty of Business and Economics. The college was established following
the implementation of the university's re-engineered business processes.

Soon after establishment, new curricula in Information System and Statistics were developed.
The first batch of Statistics students joined in 2008 (originally admitted to the Department of
Applied Mathematics and Statistics under the then Faculty of Education). The first Information
Systems students were admitted in 2009/10. The college later opened BSc programs in Information
Technology and Software Engineering based on national-level needs assessments.

The college now has **six** departments of instruction: Computer Science, Information
Technology, Information System, Information Science, Software Engineering, and Statistics. It
runs regular undergraduate (4–5 year BSc), postgraduate (MSc), and continuing education (BSc)
programs. All CCI departments share at least one common Statistics and one common computer
application course.

**Vision:** To be an internationally recognized center for teaching, research and consultancy in
the fields of Statistics and Computer Sciences and applications.

**Mission:** (aligned with Haramaya University's mission) — promote academic excellence in
teaching, research, and community engagement across all CCI departments.

## Computer Science (CS)

**Background:**

The Department of Computer Science was officially opened in 2003, beginning with an enrollment
capacity of 150 students. It graduated 450 students by the end of the 2012 academic year, and
was later restructured under the College of Computing and Informatics. The department has
modernized and expanded its infrastructure with eight computer laboratories (40 computers each)
and is a local Cisco Networking Academy offering Cisco-based networking courses.

**Objectives (paraphrased focus):**

- Concentrates on the **scientific aspects of computing** and gives students a thorough
  understanding of the underlying concepts and principles of Computer Science.
- Aims to produce graduates who can adapt to a rapidly changing field, with an entrepreneurial
  and problem-solving mindset.
- Emphasizes both theoretical depth and applicable practical skills (including emerging
  Internet-related technologies).
- Programs: **BSc in Computer Science**, **MSc in Computer Science**, **MSc in Artificial
  Intelligence** (new).

## Software Engineering (SWE)

**Background:**

The Department of Software Engineering started in 2008, under the College of Computing and
Informatics, launched in response to national demand for skilled software development
professionals. It runs a harmonized four-year curriculum and is supported by the other CCI
departments (CS, IS, ISC, STAT).

**Objectives:**

- Provide quality education and training, cultivating skills, confidence, and commitment to
  serve the country.
- Respond to the government's policy of increasing trained manpower and reduce the shortage of
  skilled workers needed by organizations.
- Provide quality community services to institutions and communities surrounding the
  university.
- Program: **BSc in Software Engineering**.

## Information Technology (IT)

**Background:**

Information Technology (IT) emerged as a distinct discipline because Computer Science programs
alone were not producing enough graduates capable of meeting organizations' practical technology
needs — troubleshooting, administration, integration, and customization of IT systems. IT is
sometimes used broadly (ICT, all of computing) and sometimes narrowly (the subject area that
meets organizations' computing-technology needs). The Ethiopian government treats ICT as an
enabling sector for national development, which motivated launching the BSc program at Haramaya.

**Vision:** To create an environment where all students, staff, and the community have easy
access to technology resources and information, and to provide infrastructure supporting the
university's strategic goals — including active participation in research/projects and community
service related to IT.

**Objectives:** Train graduates who can:

- Combine broad IT knowledge with practical skills in configuration and maintenance of ICT
  infrastructure.
- Take care of an organization's technology and infrastructure needs.
- Select, integrate, and maintain appropriate hardware/software for organizations (network
  installation/administration, web design, multimedia resources, email systems).
- Work in organizations implementing and managing automated information systems for scientific,
  educational, commercial, and other purposes.
- Plan and manage an organization's technology life cycle (maintenance, upgrades, replacement).
- Programs: **BSc in Information Technology**, **BEd in Information Technology**.

## Information System (IS)

**Background:**

There is a need for information system specialists who focus on **integrating computing
technologies and business processes** to meet organizational needs. IS graduates must understand
both technical and organizational factors, and help organizations use technology to achieve
goals efficiently and effectively. IS professionals help identify organizational information
system requirements and are involved in specification, design, and development. They must
understand organizational principles and practices well enough to **bridge the gap between
technical and management communities**.

**Mission:** Aligned with Haramaya University's mission — train competent graduates in
Information Systems, undertake knowledge-generating and problem-solving research, and provide
demand-driven, transformative community engagement contributing to sustainable development.

**Management Information Systems (MIS) emphasis** (from the department's own description):

- MIS professionals integrate IT solutions and business processes to meet organizational
  information needs.
- They understand both technical and organizational factors, playing a leading role in
  determining requirements and being active in design, implementation, and management of
  information systems.
- MIS professionals work closely with managers and serve a **bridging role** between
  organizational and computing professionals — emphasizing project management, business
  functional areas, teamwork, systems analysis and design, database design, network management,
  and organizational controls.
- The MIS program develops graduates who can function as information technology managers or
  professionals with potential for executive positions.
- Program: **BSc in Information System**.

## Information Science (ISC)

**Background:**

Haramaya University developed a baccalaureate curriculum in Information Science in 2007. The
department originally had the nomenclature "Department of Information Studies," later renamed
(after national-level curriculum review) to "Department of Information Science."

**Objectives:**

- Provide qualified, skilled, and competent information professionals to meet the country's
  education policy needs and fill scarce personnel gaps.
- Prepare information professionals with a **client-centered perspective** — designing/adapting
  information products and services responsive to user needs (rather than adapting users to
  services).
- Provide professional education for careers in **libraries, information agencies, the
  information industry**, and in business, industry, government, and research settings where
  information is a vital resource.
- Produce information specialists/librarians with theoretical knowledge and practical skill in
  modern ICTs.
- Graduate profile: librarians, systems/business analysts, database administrators, computer
  support specialists, network specialists, telecommunications analysts, Internet/web
  specialists.

**Vision:** Build a strong teaching, learning, and research environment in information science
to meet evolving global needs and equip students with current knowledge, skills, and practical
orientation.

**Mission:** Promote academic excellence in teaching, learning, research, and consultancy to
produce transformative, qualified, and competent information professionals in the country.

**Broader discipline framing (from the program-level description):**

- The role of libraries/information centers is to ensure availability and accessibility of
  information resources, requiring a client-centered perspective.
- The program provides professional education in **librarianship** for careers in libraries,
  information agencies, and the information industry.
- Objectives include producing graduates who can plan, design, develop, organize, and manage
  modern and traditional **information resource centers/libraries/agencies**; understand the
  relationship between society, people, organizations, and technology; lead research on
  information issues; and understand the historical, social, cultural, and economic dimensions
  of information work.
- Programs: **BSc in Information Science**, **MSc in Information Science**, **MSc in Data
  Science**.

## Statistics (STAT)

**Background:**

The Department of Statistics was established in the 2008/09 academic year under the College of
Computing and Informatics, with the objective of providing statistical training, research, and
consultancy services. It runs a BSc in Statistics and a postgraduate program in Econometrics.

**Objectives:** The department aims to promote statistical education in Ethiopia, specifically
to:

- Train qualified graduates in Statistics with problem-solving capacity.
- Develop critical statistical reasoning, emphasizing computational, mathematical, and
  communication skills (oral and written).
- Provide training in statistical software and statistical literacy.
- Provide statistical consultancy services.
- Participate in and lead **socioeconomic and other studies** where statistical methods apply.
- Program: **BSc in Statistics** (curriculum balances probability, inference, regression, time
  series, and econometrics; graduates work in health, agriculture, finance, business, policy,
  government, NGOs, and international organizations).

## Media / Visual Resources Review

The saved HTML pages were also checked for reusable images, icons, and photos. Findings:

- **No department-specific photos** exist in any of the seven saved pages (about, CS, SWE, IT,
  IS, ISC, STAT). There are no unique department hero images, staff photos, or department icons.
- The only branding asset present is the shared **CCI logo**:
  `https://www.haramaya.edu.et/cci/wp-content/uploads/sites/16/2026/06/cci1.png`
  (university/college-level branding, not department-specific).
- All other images found are **generic third-party plugin assets** — an accessibility widget's
  language-flag icons (English, German, Spanish, etc.) and Font Awesome UI icons (`fa-plus`,
  `fa-minus`, `fa-sun`) used for expandable tabs. None of these are relevant or meaningful for
  reuse in this project.

**Recommendation:** Do not attempt to reuse the CCI logo or any of these assets without
permission — it is Haramaya University's own branding, not open content. For this project, it is
better to design original icons/illustrations per department (e.g. simple Unicode/emoji icons
already used in `departments` data, or custom SVGs) rather than pull assets from the university
site.
