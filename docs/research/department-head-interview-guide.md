# Department Head Interview Guide
## Department Choice Guidance System - Data Collection Framework

**Purpose**: This guide structures the interviews with department heads to gather accurate, actionable data for the recommendation engine.

---

## Interview Structure

### A. Distinguishing Identity (Core of Recommender Logic)

**Question 1**: In your own words, what is the core focus of this department — what does it train students to actually do?

**Question 2**: What's the main thing that separates this department from the other CCI departments (CS/SWE/IT/IS/ISC/STAT)? Where do people most often confuse it with another?

**Question 3**: What kind of student tends to thrive here — what skills, interests, or mindset predicts success?

---

### B. Curriculum & Skill Signals

**Question 4**: What are the 2-3 defining or "hardest" courses in this department, and what kind of thinking do they require?
- Heavy math/theory?
- Hands-on building?
- Systems/infrastructure?
- Business-process focus?

**Question 5**: Roughly what balance does the program strike between theory and practical/project work?
- Theory-heavy (70%+ theoretical)
- Balanced (50/50)
- Practice-heavy (70%+ hands-on)

### D. Common Misconceptions / Advising Experience

**Question 8**: What do you find new students most often misunderstand about this department before choosing it?

**Question 9**: If a first-year student came to you unsure whether to pick this department or another, what's the first question you'd ask them to help decide?
