---

**Subject**: Request for Input: Department Choice Guidance System Development

**Message**:

Dear CCI Department Heads,

I hope this message finds you well.

I am a CCI student currently undertaking my industrial practice internship at Haramaya University ICT Center. As part of my project, I am developing a **Department Choice Guidance System** to help students make informed decisions when selecting their department after completing the freshman course.

**Project Goal**: Build an intelligent recommendation system that matches students with compatible departments (CS/SWE/IT/IS/ISC/Statistics) based on their interests, skills, and career goals.

**Why Your Input Matters**: Your expertise and insights about what distinguishes each department and what kind of students thrive in your program are critical to building an accurate recommendation engine.

**What We Need**: Deep information about your department's core focus, curriculum, career outcomes, and common student misconceptions. This will help us create meaningful guidance for incoming students.

**Time Required**: Approximately 10-15 minutes to complete the questionnaire.

**Questionnaire Link**: https://forms.gle/YYSiKPcRzfVHbk5e9

Thank you very much for your time and valuable contribution to this project!

Best regards,

Haramaya University ICT Center

## Google Form Checklist

Ensure your form includes all advisor-approved questions:

### Section A: Distinguishing Identity
- ☐ Q1: What is the core focus of your department?
- ☐ Q2: What separates your department from CS/SWE/IT/IS/ISC/STAT?
- ☐ Q3: What kind of student thrives in your department?

### Section B: Curriculum & Skill Signals
- ☐ Q4: What are 2-3 defining/hardest courses and their thinking requirements?
- ☐ Q5: Theory-to-practice balance in your program?

### Section C: Career Outcomes
- ☐ Q6: Common career paths/job roles (local & international)?
- ☐ Q7: Job market demand in Ethiopia?

### Section D: Misconceptions & Advising
- ☐ Q8: Common student misconceptions about your department?
- ☐ Q9: First question you ask undecided students?

### Additional
- ☐ Department name (dropdown)
- ☐ Respondent name and title (optional)
- ☐ Email for follow-up clarifications (optional)
- ☐ Permission to quote responses in report (yes/no)
