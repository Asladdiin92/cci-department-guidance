## 🎯 Overview

The assessment uses **20 carefully crafted questions** designed to match students with the most suitable CCI department (Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, Information System, or Statistics) based on their:

- Thinking style and problem-solving approach
- Work preferences and learning style
- Career goals and aspirations
- Technical aptitude and interests
- Comfort with mathematics and theory

**Assessment Duration:** 10-15 minutes  
**Question Format:** Multiple choice (4-5 options per question)  
**Scoring:** Weighted points system (0-100% match per department)

---

## 💡 Assessment Philosophy

### Core Principles

1. **Non-Judgmental**: No "right" or "wrong" answers
2. **Preference-Based**: Measures what students enjoy, not just ability
3. **Predictive**: Based on real patterns from department head interviews
4. **Honest**: Doesn't oversell any department
5. **Evidence-Based**: Grounded in actual curriculum and career data

### Design Goals

- **Distinguish** between commonly confused departments (CS/SWE, IT/IS)
- **Reveal** latent preferences students might not consciously recognize
- **Predict** long-term satisfaction, not just short-term interest
- **Prevent** mismatches that lead to department transfers
- **Guide** without deciding - final choice remains with the student

## 🧮 Scoring Methodology

### Point Calculation

**For each question:**
- Each option awards points to one or more departments
- Points range from 0 to +3
- Higher points = stronger alignment

**Total possible points per department:**
- Maximum: 60 points (if student selected all options favoring that department)
- Realistic range: 20-50 points

### Score Conversion

```
Match Percentage = (Department Points / 60) × 100
```

**Example:**
- Student earns 45 points for SWE
- Match Score = (45/60) × 100 = **75% match**

### Weighted Scoring (Advanced)

Different question categories can have different weights:

```
Final Score = Σ (Category Weight × Category Score)
```

**Current weights:**
- Thinking Style: 25%
- Work Preferences: 20%
- Career Goals: 20%
- Learning Style: 15%
- Technical Aptitude: 15%
- Meta-Reflection: 5%

## ✅ Validation Strategy

### Pre-Launch Validation

1. **Department Head Review**
   - Each department head reviews questions
   - Validates that scoring reflects their department accurately
   - Confirms career paths and course difficulty

2. **Pilot Testing**
   - 20-30 current students from each department
   - Compare their actual department with recommendation
   - **Target:** 70%+ accuracy

3. **Advisor Review**
   - Academic advisors review questions
   - Ensure questions are clear and unbiased
   - Validate explanations

### Post-Launch Monitoring

1. **Track Accuracy**
   - Survey students 6 months after using system
   - Ask: "Did you choose the recommended department?"
   - Ask: "Are you satisfied with your choice?"

2. **Adjust Weights**
   - If certain departments are over/under-recommended
   - Fine-tune scoring weights
   - Update question wording

3. **Add/Remove Questions**
   - Based on feedback
   - If questions are confusing or not useful
