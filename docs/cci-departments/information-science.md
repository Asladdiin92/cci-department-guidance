# Information Science (ISC)

**College:** Computing and Informatics (CCI), Haramaya University
**Source:** `https://www.haramaya.edu.et/cci/department-of-information-science/`

---

## Background

Haramaya University developed a baccalaureate curriculum in Information Science in 2007. The
department originally had the nomenclature "Department of Information Studies," later renamed
(after national-level curriculum review) to the "Department of Information Science."

## Objectives

- Provide qualified, skilled, and competent information professionals to meet the country's
  education policy needs and fill scarce personnel gaps.
- Prepare information professionals with a **client-centered perspective** — designing/adapting
  information products and services responsive to user needs (rather than adapting users to
  services).
- Provide professional education for careers in **libraries, information agencies, the
  information industry**, and in business, industry, government, and research settings where
  information is a vital resource.
- Produce information specialists/librarians with theoretical knowledge and practical skill in
  modern ICTs.
- Graduate profile: librarians, systems/business analysts, database administrators, computer
  support specialists, network specialists, telecommunications analysts, Internet/web
  specialists.

## Vision

Build a strong teaching, learning, and research environment in information science to meet
evolving global needs and equip students with current knowledge, skills, and practical
orientation.

## Mission

Promote academic excellence in teaching, learning, research, and consultancy to produce
transformative, qualified, and competent information professionals in the country.

## Broader Discipline Framing

- The role of libraries/information centers is to ensure availability and accessibility of
  information resources, requiring a client-centered perspective.
- The program provides professional education in **librarianship** for careers in libraries,
  information agencies, and the information industry.
- Objectives include producing graduates who can plan, design, develop, organize, and manage
  modern and traditional **information resource centers/libraries/agencies**; understand the
  relationship between society, people, organizations, and technology; lead research on
  information issues; and understand the historical, social, cultural, and economic dimensions
  of information work.

## Programs

- BSc in Information Science
- MSc in Information Science
- MSc in Data Science

## Signature Focus

ISC is the **library/information-science + ICT** department — information organization,
retrieval, dissemination, records/archives management, digital libraries, and client-centered
information services. Distinct from IS (which is organizational/business-process focused).

## Program Profile

The curriculum of the Information Science program is organized in the context of library and
information science; human-interaction; information generation, information organization,
information access, and information dissemination. The program offers basic general knowledge
of the field of Information Science with opportunities to specialize in particular areas in
preparation for a variety of career opportunities/outcomes.

## Graduate Profile

Libraries or information centers are essential organs for education, information,
enlightenment, recreation, and research, and are an integral part of government organizations,
NGOs, government agencies, and institutions of learning (universities, colleges, school
libraries, etc.). They are known for generating, collecting, organizing, storing, and
disseminating up-to-date, accurate, unbiased, and relevant information in print or non-print
formats to satisfy the needs of their users.

### Knowledge and Understanding

Graduates have knowledge and understanding:

- Of general and specific subjects that serve as the foundation for Information Science.
- Of various types of information resources, systems, and services.
- Of theories, principles, processes, and techniques of organization, storage, retrieval,
  dissemination, and utilization of all forms of information.
- Of theories, practices, and principles of information systems analysis, design, development,
  and management.
- Of designing, programming, implementing, and evaluating various information systems and
  multimedia resources including the Internet.
- Of computers and communication systems, including network design, development,
  implementation, and management.
- Of the basic principles of ICT in information processing techniques.

### Major Skills and Competencies

- Analyze, design, develop, implement, and manage ICT-based information systems, services, and
  solutions (organization and retrieval of information resources).
- Generate and produce tangible and usable information services and products.
- Manage information resource centers, agencies, and system development projects.
- Provide information system/technology consultancy services.
- Create and develop practical projects related to information resources and services.

## Admission Requirement

Bachelor of Science degree or equivalent in information science, library science, information
technologies, computer science, information system, management information system, or software
engineering. Admissions are processed in accordance with the provisions in the Haramaya
University Senate legislation, article 100.
