# Statistics (STAT)

**College:** Computing and Informatics (CCI), Haramaya University
**Source:** `https://www.haramaya.edu.et/cci/departemnt-of-statistics/`

---

## Background

The Department of Statistics was established in the 2008/09 academic year under the College of
Computing and Informatics, with the objective of providing statistical training, research, and
consultancy services. It runs a BSc in Statistics and a postgraduate program in Econometrics.

## Objectives

The department aims to promote statistical education in Ethiopia, specifically to:

- Train qualified graduates in Statistics with problem-solving capacity.
- Develop critical statistical reasoning, emphasizing computational, mathematical, and
  communication skills (oral and written).
- Provide training in statistical software and statistical literacy.
- Provide statistical consultancy services.
- Participate in and lead **socioeconomic and other studies** where statistical methods apply.

## Programs

- BSc in Statistics (curriculum balances probability, inference, regression, time series, and
  econometrics)

## Career Outlook

Graduates work in health, agriculture, finance, business, policy, government, NGOs, and
international organizations.

## Signature Focus

Statistics centers on **statistical reasoning, data collection/processing/analysis,
consultancy, and evidence-based decision making** — the department most oriented toward
quantitative methods and research design within CCI.

---

## Academic Program Objective

### General Objective

To train middle level statisticians in order to meet the demands of the socio-economic,
business, and development sectors in the country.

### Specific Objectives

- To train middle level statisticians who can serve in collecting, processing, and analyzing
  statistical data.
- To train middle level statisticians who can assist in statistical research undertakings and
  provision of statistical services.
- To produce middle level statisticians who can manage and supervise the activities of
  statistical units or offices at both government and non-governmental organizations.
- To produce potential candidates for graduate studies so as to meet the demand for high level
  statisticians who can teach and conduct research at HEIs, and other research and training
  centers.

---

## Admission Requirement

An applicant must have successfully completed the science preparatory in a recognized secondary
school with good performance in mathematics in the national or equivalent placement/entrance
examination, or the applicant must have graduated from a recognized university/college with a
diploma in Statistics or related fields.
