# Software Engineering (SWE)

**College:** Computing and Informatics (CCI), Haramaya University
**Source:** `https://www.haramaya.edu.et/cci/department-of-software-engineering/`

---

## Background

The Department of Software Engineering started in 2008, under the College of Computing and
Informatics, launched in response to national demand for skilled software development
professionals. It runs a harmonized four-year curriculum and is supported by the other CCI
departments (CS, IS, ISC, STAT).

## Objectives

- Provide quality education and training, cultivating skills, confidence, and commitment to
  serve the country.
- Respond to the government's policy of increasing trained manpower and reduce the shortage of
  skilled workers needed by organizations.
- Provide quality community services to institutions and communities surrounding the
  university.

## Programs

- BSc in Software Engineering

## Signature Focus

Software Engineering is the most **practice-oriented, build-first** department in CCI —
centered on the systematic design, development, testing, and maintenance of software systems,
with strong emphasis on modern frameworks and industry best practices.

---

## Program Goals

The aim of this document is to provide a curriculum for a degree program that concentrates more
on the scientific aspects in the field of Software Engineering, and also to give the students a
thorough understanding of the underlying concepts and principles pertaining to the areas in the
field of Software Engineering. By understanding these concepts, the students will have the
ability to adapt to changes in what is a rapidly changing field. Significant emphasis is also
placed on developing practical skills that will be applicable to working with computers in
Ethiopia. In particular a number of courses dealing with developing skills applicable to working
with emerging technologies related to the Internet; although Internet use is comparatively less
in Ethiopia at the moment this situation will undoubtedly change in the future.

### Educational Aims of the Program

- To enable prospective graduates to acquire the necessary knowledge and skills to meet the
  growing needs of the country for trained man power in Software Engineering.
- To produce a high quality graduate with an entrepreneurial and problem solving mind set.
- To produce highly competent students who are able to practically apply their knowledge in the
  real world system.
- To cope up the program with international standards.

## Program Learning Outcomes

Program learning outcome specifies the expectation of the program at the end of the educational
process in the Department of Software Engineering. This clearly states what students are able
to perform at the end of successful completion of the course work, focusing on the achievable
output unlike the intention described as part of the program aim.

---

## Graduate Profile

Graduates of the program will be able to:

- Make rational and justified decisions regarding the selection of methods, tools, and
  techniques for the development of an application.
- Design, develop, and maintain good quality software by applying software engineering
  principles.
- Select the appropriate software model to develop software based on the type of project.
- Design test cases for an application and test it either by manual testing or automation
  testing using appropriate testing tools.
- Design and develop advanced websites using client-side and server-side scripting languages.
- Design, develop, and maintain database systems.
- Assist the software industry in producing good quality software by applying software quality
  techniques.
