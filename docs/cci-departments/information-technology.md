## Background

Information Technology (IT) emerged as a distinct discipline because Computer Science programs
alone were not producing enough graduates capable of meeting organizations' practical technology
needs — troubleshooting, administration, integration, and customization of IT systems. IT is
sometimes used broadly (ICT, all of computing) and sometimes narrowly (the subject area that
meets organizations' computing-technology needs). The Ethiopian government treats ICT as an
enabling sector for national development, which motivated launching the BSc program at Haramaya.

## Vision

To create an environment where all students, staff, and the community have easy access to
technology resources and information, and to provide infrastructure supporting the university's
strategic goals — including active participation in research/projects and community service
related to IT.

## Objectives

Train graduates who can:

- Combine broad IT knowledge with practical skills in configuration and maintenance of ICT
  infrastructure.
- Take care of an organization's technology and infrastructure needs.
- Select, integrate, and maintain appropriate hardware/software for organizations (network
  installation/administration, web design, multimedia resources, email systems).
- Work in organizations implementing and managing automated information systems for scientific,
  educational, commercial, and other purposes.
- Plan and manage an organization's technology life cycle (maintenance, upgrades, replacement).

## Programs

- BSc in Information Technology
- BEd in Information Technology

## Signature Focus

IT is the **infrastructure and operations** department — networking, systems administration,
security, and keeping technology running reliably for organizations, distinct from CS's
theoretical focus and SWE's software-product focus.

## Graduation Requirements

The major graduation requirements for the Regular and CEP program include:

- Successful completion of all courses (242 ECTS).
- A Cumulative Grade Point Average (C.G.P.A) of at least 2.00.
- A Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of at least 2.00 in major courses.
- At least a C grade in the Industrial Project courses.
- No grade of "F" in any of the courses.

Admission requirements would be similar to the general institutional requirements. That is, the
student should complete preparatory level education and the entry point is determined
accordingly by the Ministry of Education for regular students, whereas the admission criteria
for distance and continuing education programs, as well as for the summer education program,
are as per the University's requirement.
